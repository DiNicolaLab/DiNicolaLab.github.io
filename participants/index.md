## For Participants

Thank you for your interest in taking part in research with the NEST Lab! We study how the brain and mind change across major life transitions, such as adolescence and pregnancy. Our studies may include MRI scans, cognitive tasks, questionnaires, and short surveys completed on your phone over several weeks.

Information about current studies is coming soon. In the meantime, if you are interested in participating, please contact us at nestlab (at) virginia.edu.
