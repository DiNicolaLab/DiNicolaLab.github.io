    <h3 class="study-title">Maternal Cognition in Context Project</h3>
    <p>We aim to better understand how cognition (e.g., memory, attention) might change across pregnancy and postpartum, including in relation to factors like mood and sleep. We are recruiting participants who are pregnant, aim to become pregnant, or have a pregnant partner, as well as control participants.</p>

    <p>If you choose to join this study:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Your participation can last up to 15 months (if you choose).</li>
      <li>You will be asked to install an application on your smartphone.</li>
      <li>For seven days each month, you will receive notifications to complete phone surveys and cognitive games twice per day (in the morning &amp; evening).</li>
      <li>You will also have the opportunity to record an audio diary about events of your day.</li>
      <li>You do not need to be local to UVA or come into the lab – this study can be completed wherever you are!</li>
    </ul>

    <p>If interested in joining, please use the QR code on the flyer to the left to complete a brief survey! For any questions, please email nestlab@virginia.edu.</p>
