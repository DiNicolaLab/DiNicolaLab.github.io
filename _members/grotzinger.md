description: "Dr. Grotzinger is a Postdoctoral Research Associate using neuroimaging methods to study the dynamic changes that occur in the brain across the perinatal transition. She completed her PhD in Psychological and Brain Sciences in 2026 at the University of California, Santa Barbara, under the guidance of Prof. Emily Jacobs. Her previous dissertation work focused on using MRI to map the morphological and cerebrovascular adaptations to pregnancy across the gestational window and early postpartum. In the NEST Lab, Hannah will apply an individual-specific approach to probe changes in brain function and connectivity across pregnancy and adolescence. She is also interested in exploring the relationship between adverse conditions of pregnancy (e.g., perinatal depression, preeclampsia, etc.) and their impact on brain function, structure, and long-term health. In her free time, Hannah enjoys training for triathlons, baking gluten-free goodies, and cuddling with her one-eyed orange tabby cat, Colin."
