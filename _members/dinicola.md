description: "Dr. DiNicola joined UVA's Psychology Department as an Assistant Professor in 2025. Dr. DiNicola's research grapples with how the human brain supports complex cognitive functions. Understanding how we engage in advanced forms of thought, like memory and social inference, can provide insight into why human minds are flexible but also vulnerable to psychopathology. In particular, the NEST Lab uses within-individual ('precision') approaches to study association networks – how they are organized and the processes they support. The lab is also increasingly interested in how the brain changes across periods of life featuring major hormonal transitions, including adolescence and pregnancy/postpartum."
